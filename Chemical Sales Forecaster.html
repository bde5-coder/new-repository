How the forecast works

Holt-Winters tracks level, trend and 12-month seasonality, with parameters tuned per product and a damped trend so long horizons don't run away. Trend × seasonal index fits a straight-line trend and scales it by each calendar month's typical share. Last year + YoY growth repeats the last 12 months, scaled by the most recent year-on-year change.

In Auto mode each method is backtested on the last 6 months of history and the one with the lowest error (MAPE) is used for that product. The confidence band comes from that backtest error and widens with distance.

Scenario maths: price change = planned price move + feedstock change × pass-through. Volume = statistical forecast × (1 + demand shift) × (1 + elasticity × price change), capped at capacity. Revenue = volume × (average price of the last 3 months × (1 + price change)).
